How Often and How Much to Water Succulents

Quick Answer: Water your succulents when the soil is completely dry, typically every 1-3 weeks, depending on the season and conditions.

How Often to Water

Succulents are drought-resistant plants that require minimal watering. Generally, you should water them every 1 to 3 weeks. The frequency of watering can vary based on the environment—hot, dry areas will need more frequent watering than cooler, humid environments.

How to Check Soil Moisture

To check if your succulent needs water, insert your finger into the soil up to your second knuckle. If the soil feels dry at that depth, it’s time to water. Alternatively, you can use a moisture meter for a more precise reading.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

Understanding the signs can help you maintain the health of your succulents:

  • Overwatering: Yellowing leaves, mushy stems, and root rot.
  • Underwatering: Wrinkled, shriveling leaves, and dry, brittle stems.

Seasonal Watering Schedule

Season Watering Frequency
Spring Every 1-2 weeks
Summer Every week
Fall Every 2-3 weeks
Winter Every 3-4 weeks

Common Watering Mistakes

Avoid these common pitfalls when watering your succulents:

  • Watering on a schedule rather than checking soil moisture.
  • Using pots without drainage holes.
  • Watering too much or too frequently.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use tap water for my succulents?

Yes, but let it sit for 24 hours to allow chlorine to evaporate, which can be harmful to your plants.

2. What type of soil is best for succulents?

Use a well-draining soil mix, specifically formulated for cacti and succulents.

3. Should I mist succulents?

No, succulents prefer to be watered directly at the base. Misting can lead to rot.
