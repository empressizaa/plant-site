How Often to Water Wandering Jew

Quick Answer: Water your Wandering Jew when the top inch of soil is dry. Typically, this is every 1-2 weeks depending on the season and humidity levels.

How Often to Water

Wandering Jew plants thrive in well-draining soil and prefer to dry out slightly between waterings. Generally, watering every 1 to 2 weeks is advisable, but this can vary based on indoor or outdoor conditions.

How to Check Soil Moisture

To determine if your Wandering Jew needs water, stick your finger into the soil up to the first knuckle. If it feels dry, it's time to water. If it’s still moist, wait a few days before checking again.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

Overwatering:

  • Yellowing leaves
  • Wilting despite wet soil
  • Soft, mushy stems

Underwatering:

  • Leaf droop
  • Wrinkled or curled leaves
  • Brown leaf tips

Seasonal Watering Schedule

Season Watering Frequency
Spring Every 1 week
Summer Every 1 week
Fall Every 2 weeks
Winter Every 2-3 weeks

Common Watering Mistakes

  • Watering on a fixed schedule without checking soil moisture.
  • Allowing the pot to sit in standing water.
  • Using heavy, dense soil that retains too much moisture.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I mist my Wandering Jew?

Yes, misting can help maintain humidity, especially in dry environments, but it is not a substitute for regular watering.

2. Should I use distilled water?

While distilled water is not necessary, using it can prevent the buildup of minerals in the soil.

3. Is it normal for leaves to drop occasionally?

Yes, some leaf drop is normal, particularly if the plant experiences stress from overwatering or underwatering.

4. How can I ensure proper drainage?

Ensure your pot has drainage holes and consider using a well-draining potting mix.

5. What should I do if I overwatered my plant?

If you suspect overwatering, reduce waterings and allow the soil to dry out completely to avoid root rot.
