How Often to Water Kalanchoe

Quick Answer: Water Kalanchoe plants when the top inch of soil feels dry. This typically translates to every 2-3 weeks, depending on the environment.

How Often to Water

Kalanchoe plants prefer to dry out between waterings. In general, watering every two to three weeks is ideal for these succulents. However, it's essential to adjust your watering schedule based on light conditions, humidity, and temperature.

How to Check Soil Moisture

Before watering your Kalanchoe, check the soil moisture. Insert your finger about an inch into the soil. If it feels dry at this depth, it's time to water. If it's still moist, wait a few days before checking again.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

Understanding the signs of overwatering and underwatering is crucial for the health of your Kalanchoe:

  • Overwatering: Yellowing leaves, wilting, and root rot.
  • Underwatering: Shriveling leaves, dry leaf edges, and stunted growth.

Seasonal Watering Schedule

Season Watering Frequency
Spring Every 2-3 weeks

Common Watering Mistakes

Avoid these common mistakes when watering your Kalanchoe:

  • Using cold water straight from the tap; always use room temperature water.
  • Watering without checking soil moisture; feel the soil first.
  • Letting the plant sit in standing water; ensure proper drainage.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use succulent fertilizer while watering?

Yes, use a half-strength succulent fertilizer during the growing season to promote healthy growth.

2. How can I tell if my Kalanchoe is getting too much sun?

If the leaves start to burn or develop brown spots, it might be receiving too much direct sunlight.

3. Is it normal for Kalanchoe leaves to drop?

Yes, some leaf drop can occur, especially for lower leaves, but excessive dropping could indicate stress from over or underwatering.

4. Should I water my Kalanchoe during dormancy?

Water sparingly during dormancy, typically less frequently than during the growing season.
