How Often to Water Kalanchoe

Quick Answer: Water your Kalanchoe every 2-3 weeks, allowing the soil to dry out completely between waterings.

How Often to Water

Kalanchoe plants thrive in bright light and prefer infrequent but thorough watering. Generally, it is recommended to water Kalanchoe every 2 to 3 weeks, checking the soil moisture first, as overwatering can lead to root rot.

How to Check Soil Moisture

To check if your Kalanchoe needs watering, insert your finger about an inch deep into the soil. If it feels dry at that depth, it’s time to water. If it’s still moist, wait another few days before checking again.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

Overwatering Signs:

  • Yellowing leaves
  • Soft, mushy stems
  • Root rot

Underwatering Signs:

  • Wrinkled or shriveled leaves
  • Leaf drop
  • Slow growth

Seasonal Watering Schedule

Season Frequency
Spring Every 2 weeks
Summer Every 1-2 weeks
Fall Every 2-3 weeks
Winter Every 3-4 weeks

Common Watering Mistakes

Some common mistakes to avoid when watering Kalanchoe include:

  • Watering too frequently without checking soil moisture
  • Using shallow pots that dry out quickly
  • Allowing the plant to sit in water

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I water my Kalanchoe with ice cubes?

While it may seem convenient, using ice cubes can lead to uneven watering and temperature shock. It's best to use room temperature water.

2. How do I revive an overwatered Kalanchoe?

If you've overwatered, allow the soil to dry out completely and consider repotting the plant in fresh soil if root rot is detected.

3. Is it normal for Kalanchoe leaves to drop?

Leaf drop can occur due to environmental stress, including incorrect watering. Make sure to check the care conditions if this happens.
