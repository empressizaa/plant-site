What Is Plant Population?

Plant population is the number of plants grown within a defined area. It is commonly expressed as plants per acre, plants per hectare, plants per square foot, or plants per square meter.

What Is Plant Density?

Plant density describes how closely plants are distributed within a growing area. Plant spacing and row spacing determine how much space is allocated to each plant.

How to Calculate Plant Population

Plant population can be estimated by dividing the growing area by the amount of space allocated to each plant.

Rectangular planting:

Plant population = Growing area ÷ (Plant spacing × Row spacing)

Triangular planting:

Triangular or hexagonal arrangements can provide approximately 13.4% greater theoretical density than a rectangular arrangement using the same spacing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate plant population?

Select the appropriate plant category or crop type, enter your growing area, and provide the plant and row spacing. The calculator estimates the plant population.

How many plants can I grow per acre?

The number of plants per acre depends on the plant spacing and row spacing. Enter those measurements to calculate the estimated population per acre.

How many plants can I grow per hectare?

Plants per hectare depend on the spacing used. Enter the plant and row spacing to calculate the estimated population per hectare.

What is a plant density calculator?

A plant density calculator estimates how many plants can occupy a particular area based on the space allocated to each plant.

Can I calculate the land needed for a certain number of plants?

Yes. Enter your target number of plants and the spacing you intend to use. The calculator estimates the required land in acres, hectares, square feet, and square meters.

Can I use this calculator for different crops?

Yes. The calculator supports broad plant and crop categories and allows you to enter specific spacing when a particular crop or variety requires different spacing.

What if I don't know my plant spacing?

Select the closest plant or crop type when an automatic recommendation is available. You can also enter a specific plant name or manually provide the recommended spacing for your crop.

Does plant population depend on spacing?

Yes. Smaller spacing generally allows more plants to occupy an area, while wider spacing generally results in fewer plants.
