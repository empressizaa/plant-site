How Often to Water Grass in 90-Degree Weather

In 90-degree weather, grass typically requires watering every 1 to 3 days, aiming for 1 to 1.5 inches of water per week depending on the type of grass and soil conditions.

How Often to Water

Grass needs more frequent watering in hot weather. In general, consider watering every 1 to 2 days, focusing on the morning hours to reduce evaporation. Adjust based on rainfall and humidity levels to prevent overwatering.

How to Check Soil Moisture

To determine if your grass needs water, use a soil moisture meter or conduct a simple test with your finger. Insert your finger into the soil about an inch deep. If it feels dry at that depth, it’s time to water.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

  • Overwatering: Yellowing leaves, mushy soil, or a musty smell.
  • Underwatering: Wilting, browning tips, and dry soil that pulls away from the edges of the planter.

Common Watering Mistakes

  • Watering too frequently without giving the soil time to dry.
  • Not adjusting watering schedules based on rainfall.
  • Watering late in the day, leading to disease due to extended moisture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I water my grass at night? A: It's better to water in the morning to prevent disease.
Q: Should I use a sprinkler or a hose? A: Both are effective, but ensure even coverage with either method.
Q: How can I tell what type of grass I have? A: Look for characteristics like leaf shape and color or consult local extension services.
