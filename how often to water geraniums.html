how often to water geraniums

Direct Answer: Water your geraniums (Pelargonium spp.) roughly every 7 to 10 days depending on the season, or as soon as the top 2 inches of soil feel dry.

How to Check the Soil Before Hydrating Your Geraniums

Never water on a strict calendar schedule. Instead, follow these steps to see if your geraniums actually need moisture:

  • The Knuckle Test: Insert your finger two inches deep into the soil mix. If it feels bone dry, it is time to water.
  • The Weight Test: Pick up the pot. Dry soil is significantly lighter than wet soil.

Signs of Overwatering vs. Underwatering

An improper moisture routine can cause severe damage. Watch out for these warning signs:

  • Overwatered Signs: Yellowing lower leaves, mushy base stems, and fungus gnats in the soil.
  • Underwatered Signs: Crispy brown leaf tips, drooping stems, and soil pulling away from the edges of the pot.

By monitoring these physical signs and checking the soil depth regularly, you can keep your plant thriving in any indoor environment.
