How Often to Water Succulents

Quick Answer: Water succulents every 1-3 weeks, allowing the soil to completely dry out between waterings.

How Often to Water

Generally, succulents should be watered every 1 to 3 weeks. The exact frequency depends on the climate, the season, and the environment in which they are kept. In hotter months, you may need to water more frequently, while in cooler months, less water is needed.

How to Check Soil Moisture

The best way to check if your succulent needs water is to stick your finger about an inch into the soil. If it feels dry, it's time to water. Alternatively, you can use a moisture meter for a more accurate reading.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

Overwatering:

  • Yellowing leaves
  • Soft, mushy leaves
  • Leaves falling off

Underwatering:

  • Wrinkled or shriveled leaves
  • Leaves falling off
  • Pale color

Seasonal Watering Schedule

Season Watering Frequency
Spring Once a week
Summer Once every 1-2 weeks
Fall Once every 2-3 weeks
Winter Once every 3-4 weeks

Common Watering Mistakes

  • Watering too frequently without allowing the soil to dry.
  • Using a pot without drainage holes.
  • Neglecting to adjust water habits based on seasonal changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use tap water for my succulents?

It's best to use filtered or distilled water if possible, as tap water may contain chemicals that are not beneficial for succulents.

2. What type of soil is best for succulents?

Use a well-draining potting mix specifically designed for succulents or cacti.

3. How do I know when to repot my succulents?

If the roots are growing out of the drainage holes or the plant looks too large for its pot, it's time to repot.

4. Can succulents survive in low light?

Most succulents prefer bright, indirect light; however, some can tolerate lower light conditions but will grow more slowly.

5. Is misting a good way to water succulents?

No, misting is not recommended as it can lead to rot. It's better to water them directly at the soil level.
