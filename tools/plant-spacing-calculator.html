How to Use the Plant Spacing Calculator

Enter the plant name and the dimensions of your planting area. If the plant is available in our database, its typical spacing will automatically be entered for you.

If the plant is not recognized, you can manually enter the plant-to-plant and row-to-row spacing recommended for that plant.

This means the calculator can be used for vegetables, fruits, herbs, flowers, houseplants, shrubs, trees, succulents and many other types of plants.

What Is Plant Spacing?

Plant spacing is the distance between individual plants. Row spacing is the distance between rows of plants.

Correct spacing gives plants enough room for their roots, foliage, sunlight, airflow and mature growth.

Plant Spacing Formula

Rectangular planting:

Estimated plants = Garden area ÷ (plant spacing × row spacing)

Triangular planting:

Triangular planting can provide approximately 15.5% greater theoretical density than a rectangular grid when equal spacing is used.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many plants can I fit in my garden?

Enter your garden length and width and then enter the plant you want to grow. The calculator estimates how many plants can fit based on the plant's spacing.

Can I use this calculator for any plant?

Yes. The calculator contains spacing information for many common plants and also provides a manual spacing option for plants that are not yet in the database.

What if the calculator doesn't recognize my plant?

You can still use the calculator. Simply enter the recommended plant-to-plant spacing and row spacing for that plant manually.

Can I calculate plants per acre?

Yes. The calculator estimates the number of plants per acre based on the spacing you enter.

Can I calculate plants per hectare?

Yes. The calculator also estimates plants per hectare.

What is triangular plant spacing?

Triangular spacing offsets plants between neighboring rows. This arrangement can provide a higher theoretical planting density than a rectangular grid.
