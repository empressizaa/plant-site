How to Use the Plant Spacing Calculator

Enter the length and width of your planting area, select the measurement unit, and then enter the distance you want between individual plants and between rows.

You can use this calculator for virtually any type of plant. Simply use the spacing recommended for the plant you are growing.

For example, if a plant should be placed 12 inches apart with rows 18 inches apart, enter 12 inches as the plant spacing and 18 inches as the row spacing.

What Is Plant Spacing?

Plant spacing is the distance between individual plants. Row spacing is the distance between rows of plants.

Both measurements are important because plants need enough room for their roots, foliage, mature size, sunlight exposure, and airflow.

Plant Spacing Formula

Rectangular planting:

Estimated plants = Garden area ÷ (plant spacing × row spacing)

Triangular planting:

Triangular planting uses an approximately 13.4% higher planting density than a rectangular grid when the same spacing is used.

Rectangular vs Triangular Plant Spacing

In rectangular planting, plants are arranged in straight rows with consistent distances between plants and rows.

In triangular or hexagonal planting, plants in alternating rows are offset. This can allow more plants to fit into the same area when appropriate spacing is maintained.

Why Proper Plant Spacing Matters

Correct spacing helps prevent plants from competing excessively for light, water, nutrients, and growing space. It can also improve airflow and make watering, harvesting, pruning, and other maintenance easier.

The ideal spacing depends on the plant. Always check the recommended mature spacing for the specific plant before planting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many plants can I fit in my garden?

Enter your garden's length and width along with the recommended plant and row spacing. The calculator will estimate how many plants can fit in the available area.

Can I use this calculator for any plant?

Yes. The calculator does not require a specific plant type. Enter the recommended spacing for whatever plant you are growing.

What if I don't know my plant spacing?

Check the growing instructions for your particular plant to find its recommended spacing. You can then enter that measurement into the calculator.

Can I calculate plants per acre?

Yes. The calculator provides an estimated plants-per-acre figure based on the spacing you enter.

Can I calculate plants per hectare?

Yes. The calculator also provides an estimated plants-per-hectare figure.

What is triangular plant spacing?

Triangular spacing offsets plants between neighboring rows rather than placing every plant directly beside another plant. This can increase planting density compared with a simple rectangular grid.
