How to Calculate Soil for a Raised Garden Bed

To calculate the amount of soil needed for a raised garden bed, multiply the length by the width and then multiply by the soil depth. The calculator automatically converts different units so you can use feet, inches, meters, or centimeters.

Soil Volume = Length × Width × Soil Depth

How Much Soil Does a 4 × 8 Raised Bed Need?

A 4 × 8 foot raised bed filled to a depth of 12 inches requires 32 cubic feet of soil before adding extra soil for settling.

With a 10% settling allowance, the amount becomes 35.2 cubic feet.

How Many Bags of Soil Do You Need?

The number of bags depends on the total soil volume and the size of each bag. The calculator rounds up to the next whole bag because soil is normally purchased in complete bags.

How Deep Should a Raised Garden Bed Be?

Many vegetables can grow successfully with around 12 inches of soil, while crops with deeper root systems may benefit from additional depth. The ideal depth also depends on whether the bed is open to the ground or has a solid bottom.

Raised Bed Soil Mix

A raised bed can be filled with different combinations of topsoil, compost, peat moss, coco coir, and other materials. Because gardeners use many different recipes, the soil mix section of this calculator is optional and flexible.

You can enter only the materials you want to calculate. The calculator does not require the percentages to total 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions About Raised Bed Soil

How much soil do I need for a raised bed?

The amount depends on the raised bed's length, width, and soil depth. Multiply the three dimensions after converting them to the same unit.

How much soil does a 4 × 8 raised bed need?

A 4 × 8 foot raised bed with 12 inches of soil requires 32 cubic feet before adding a settling allowance.

How many bags of soil do I need?

Enter your raised bed dimensions and select the size of the soil bags you plan to purchase. The calculator will estimate the number of whole bags required.

Should I add extra soil for settling?

Soil can settle after a raised bed is filled. Adding around 10% extra can provide material to compensate for settling and topping up the bed.

Can I calculate soil without using a soil mix?

Yes. The soil mix section is completely optional. You can leave all percentages at 0% and calculate the total amount of soil needed.

Do the soil mix percentages have to equal 100%?

No. This calculator does not require the percentages to equal 100%. It calculates the amount represented by each percentage you enter and still provides the total soil requirement.

Can I use different units for each dimension?

Yes. For example, you can enter the length and width in feet and the soil depth in inches.

Can I calculate soil for multiple raised beds?

Yes. Enter the dimensions of one bed and the number of identical beds you are filling.
