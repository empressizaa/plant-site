How to Calculate Soil for a Raised Garden Bed

To calculate the amount of soil needed for a raised garden bed, multiply the bed's length by its width and then multiply the result by the soil depth.

Soil Volume = Length × Width × Soil Depth

The three dimensions must be converted to the same unit before calculating the volume. This calculator automatically converts feet, inches, meters, and centimeters into feet before calculating the final volume.

How Much Soil Does a 4 × 8 Raised Bed Need?

A 4 × 8 foot raised bed filled with 12 inches of soil has a base volume of 32 cubic feet.

If you add a 10% settling allowance, the calculator estimates approximately 35.2 cubic feet of soil.

How Many Bags of Soil Do You Need?

The number of bags depends on the total volume of soil required and the size of the bags you are purchasing. For example, a 35.2 cubic foot requirement would need 18 bags if each bag contains 2 cubic feet, because you need to round up to a whole bag.

How Deep Should a Raised Garden Bed Be?

Many common vegetables can grow well with around 12 inches of soil, while crops with deeper root systems may benefit from greater depth. The ideal depth also depends on whether the raised bed is open to the ground underneath or has a solid bottom.

What Soil Should You Use in a Raised Bed?

Raised beds are commonly filled with quality topsoil combined with organic matter such as compost. Some gardeners also use peat moss or coco coir depending on their preferred soil structure and moisture characteristics.

The ideal mixture depends on what you are growing, your climate, drainage, and the condition of the existing soil.

Should You Add Extra Soil for Settling?

Soil can settle after a raised bed is filled. Organic materials may also break down over time, reducing the volume slightly. Adding around 10% extra soil is a useful allowance for many raised-bed projects.

Frequently Asked Questions About Raised Bed Soil

How much soil do I need for a raised bed?

The amount depends on the raised bed's length, width, and soil depth. Multiply the three dimensions after converting them to the same unit. This calculator handles the conversion for you.

How many bags of soil do I need for a raised bed?

The number of bags depends on the total soil volume and the volume contained in each bag. Enter your bag size to calculate the number of bags required.

How much soil does a 4 × 8 raised bed need?

A 4 × 8 foot raised bed with 12 inches of soil requires 32 cubic feet before adding a settling allowance.

How many cubic yards of soil do I need?

One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet. Divide the required cubic feet by 27 to convert the amount to cubic yards.

Should I add extra soil for settling?

Adding around 10% extra soil can help compensate for settling. You can adjust the allowance in the calculator depending on your preference.

Can I calculate soil for multiple raised beds?

Yes. Enter the dimensions of one raised bed and the number of identical beds you are filling. The calculator will multiply the total soil requirement accordingly.

Can I use inches for soil depth and feet for the bed dimensions?

Yes. The calculator allows separate units for length, width, and soil depth. For example, you can enter 8 feet for length, 4 feet for width, and 12 inches for depth.
