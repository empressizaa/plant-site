How to Use the Rainwater Collection Calculator

Enter the length and width of the roof or other catchment area. You can enter the dimensions in feet or meters.

Enter the amount of rainfall you want to analyze. You can use rainfall from a single storm, month, season, or year depending on what you want to estimate.

Select the roof material to use a typical runoff coefficient, or choose a custom coefficient if you have a more appropriate value for your particular collection system.

The basic rainwater harvesting calculation is based on catchment area, rainfall depth, and a runoff coefficient. In metric units, 1 mm of rain falling on 1 square meter represents 1 liter before collection losses.

Rainwater Collection Formula

The calculator estimates collectible water using:

Collectable Rainwater = Catchment Area × Rainfall × Runoff Coefficient × System Efficiency

The runoff coefficient accounts for the portion of rainfall that actually becomes runoff available to the collection system. Roof material, surface characteristics, evaporation, splash, absorption, and other losses can affect the coefficient.

What Roof Area Should You Use?

For a basic roof catchment calculation, use the roof's horizontal footprint or plan area. This represents the area covered by the roof when viewed from above. Rain falls vertically, so the horizontal catchment area is generally the appropriate starting point for estimating rainfall captured.

Can a Rainwater Tank Hold All the Water?

Not necessarily. If the estimated rainfall collection exceeds the tank's capacity, some water may overflow unless additional storage is available. Enter your tank capacity to compare the estimated collection with the storage available.

Tank sizing should consider rainfall patterns and water demand rather than relying only on one rainfall event.

Frequently Asked Questions About Rainwater Collection

How much rainwater can I collect from my roof?

It depends on the roof catchment area, rainfall amount, and runoff coefficient. Larger roofs and higher rainfall generally produce more potential collected water.

What is a rainwater catchment calculator?

A rainwater catchment calculator estimates how much rainfall can be collected from a roof or other catchment surface.

What is a rain harvest calculator?

A rain harvest calculator is another way of describing a tool that estimates the amount of rainwater that can be harvested or collected.

Can I calculate rainwater collected from one storm?

Yes. Enter the rainfall depth for the storm and the calculator will estimate the potential collectible water.

Can I use annual rainfall?

Yes. Annual rainfall can be entered to estimate potential yearly rainwater collection.

Can I use this as a rain capture calculator?

Yes. Rain capture, rainwater capture, rainwater collection, rainwater catchment, and rain harvesting all describe closely related calculations based on the amount of precipitation falling on a catchment area.

Can rainwater be used for drinking?

This calculator only estimates quantity. Water intended for drinking requires appropriate collection, filtration, disinfection, storage, and water-quality assessment. Do not assume collected rainwater is safe to drink simply because it has been calculated or filtered.
