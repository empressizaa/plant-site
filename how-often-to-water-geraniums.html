How Often to Water Geraniums

Quick Answer: Water geraniums when the top inch of soil feels dry, typically every 5 to 10 days depending on the season and environmental conditions.

How Often to Water

Geraniums thrive best with a consistent watering schedule. Water these plants deeply but infrequently. It's important to let the soil partially dry out between waterings to prevent root rot.

How to Check Soil Moisture

To check if your geraniums need water, insert your finger into the soil up to the first knuckle. If the soil feels dry at that depth, it's time to water. For more accuracy, a moisture meter can also be used.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

Understanding the signs of overwatering and underwatering can help maintain your geraniums' health:

  • Overwatering: Yellowing leaves, wilting, and root rot.
  • Underwatering: Drooping leaves, dry soil, and browning leaf edges.

Seasonal Watering Schedule

Season Watering Frequency
Spring Every 5-7 days
Summer Every 3-5 days
Fall Every 7-10 days
Winter Every 10-14 days

Common Watering Mistakes

Here are some common mistakes to avoid when watering geraniums:

  • Watering too frequently without allowing soil to dry.
  • Poor drainage in pots or soil.
  • Ignoring changes in environmental conditions, like humidity and temperature.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I water geraniums from below?

Yes, watering from below can help prevent fungal diseases and promote deeper root growth. Just ensure the excess water can drain away.

2. How do I know if my geranium is getting too much sun?

If the leaves start to scorch or turn brown, it may be receiving too much direct sunlight. Consider moving it to a spot with filtered light.

3. Do geraniums need fertilizer when watering?

Using a balanced fertilizer every 4-6 weeks during the growing season can help promote healthy growth.
