How Often to Change Goldfish Water Without Filter

Quick Answer: Change 10-15% of your goldfish water every week. If you're not using a filter, a larger water change of 25-50% every other day may be necessary to maintain water quality.

How Often to Change Water

Without a filter, you should perform partial water changes more frequently to ensure that your goldfish remain healthy. Generally, change 10-15% of the water weekly. In absence of filtration, however, it's advisable to carry out larger changes of about 25-50% every few days.

How to Check Water Quality

Regularly test the water for ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate levels using a freshwater aquarium water test kit. Ideal parameters for goldfish include:

  • Ammonia: 0 ppm
  • Nitrite: 0 ppm
  • Nitrate: below 40 ppm

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

In the context of goldfish, consider the following signs:

  • Overwatering: Excess waste build-up, murky water, and algae blooms.
  • Underwatering: Stagnant water, a strong odor, and stressed or lethargic fish.

Common Watering Mistakes

  • Not changing the water frequently enough.
  • Using tap water without treating it for chlorine and chloramines.
  • Overfeeding goldfish, which leads to increased waste and poor water quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use bottled water for my goldfish?

It’s best to use dechlorinated tap water or treated water meant for aquariums rather than bottled water, as it may lack essential minerals and may not be suitable long-term.

2. How do I make sure my goldfish water is dechlorinated?

Let tap water sit for 24 hours to allow chlorine to dissipate, or use a dechlorinator product available at pet stores.

3. Will my goldfish be okay if I forget to change the water for a week?

It’s important to not let it go for too long. If you miss a week, change a significant amount of the water immediately to restore quality.
