How Often to Water Calathea

Water your Calathea approximately once a week, allowing the top inch of soil to dry out between waterings.

How Often to Water

The frequency of watering a Calathea can depend on various factors such as the season, humidity levels, and the size of the pot. Generally, water your Calathea every 5 to 7 days. In summer, when growth is more active, you may need to water more frequently, while in winter, you can extend this frequency as the plant's growth slows down.

How to Check Soil Moisture

Insert your finger into the soil about an inch deep. If the soil feels dry at this depth, it's time to water. Alternatively, you can use a moisture meter for a more accurate reading. Calathea enjoys evenly moist soil, but it should not be waterlogged.

Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

Identifying whether your Calathea is overwatered or underwatered is crucial for its health:

  • Overwatering: Yellowing leaves, wilting, and browning tips on the leaves are common signs.
  • Underwatering: Crispy edges, curling leaves, and drooping foliage indicate insufficient water.

Common Watering Mistakes

Avoid these common pitfalls to keep your Calathea thriving:

  • Using cold water: Use room temperature or lukewarm water to prevent shocking the plant.
  • Overwatering: Ensure pots have drainage holes and only water when the topsoil is dry to touch.
  • Ignoring humidity: Calathea loves humidity, so consider using a humidity tray or misting.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use tap water for my Calathea?
Yes, but it's best to let it sit out for 24 hours to allow chlorine to dissipate.
2. What type of soil should I use?
A well-draining potting mix is ideal, preferably one that retains some moisture.
3. How can I increase humidity for my Calathea?
Use a humidifier, place a tray of water near the plant, or cluster several plants together.
